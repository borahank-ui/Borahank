# Industry Benchmark Ratios

**Source note:** These are general industry guidelines. For live benchmarks, compare against company-specific peer groups.

## Cross-Sector Reference Points

### When to Be Concerned

| Signal | Threshold |
|--------|-----------|
| Net Debt/EBITDA | > 4× (non-financial) |
| Interest Coverage | < 2× |
| Current Ratio | < 1.0 |
| Quick Ratio | < 0.7 |
| Operating Cash Flow | Negative for 2+ years |
| Goodwill / Total Assets | > 40% |
| Receivables Days | Growing > 20% faster than revenue |
| Gross Margin decline | > 300bps YoY without explanation |

### Investment Grade vs. High Yield Credit Rough Thresholds

| Rating Category | Net Debt/EBITDA | Interest Coverage |
|----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Investment Grade (BBB+) | < 2.0× | > 5× |
| Investment Grade (BBB) | < 3.0× | > 3.5× |
| High Yield (BB) | < 4.5× | > 2.5× |
| High Yield (B) | < 6.0× | > 1.5× |
| Distressed (CCC) | > 6.0× | < 1.5× |
