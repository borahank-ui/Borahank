# Job Application Skill

This plugin enables Claude to help you craft tailored, high-quality job application materials that stand out.

## What It Does

The skill activates when you need help applying for a job. It covers:

- **Cover letters** — Specific, confident, and tailored to the role and company; never generic
- **Resume tailoring** — Reordered and reworded bullets that mirror the job description and quantify impact
- **Interview preparation** — STAR-format behavioral stories, technical question strategies, and smart questions to ask
- **Application strategy** — Identifying your strongest selling points and how to position them across every touchpoint

## How to Use It

Provide Claude with:
1. The **job description** (paste the full text or a link)
2. Your **background** (resume, LinkedIn, or a brief summary of your experience)
3. What you need: cover letter, resume bullets, interview prep, or all of the above

### Example prompts

```
Help me write a cover letter for this senior software engineer role at Stripe. Here's the job description: [paste] and here's my resume: [paste]
```

```
Tailor my resume bullets for this product manager role. The JD emphasizes data-driven decision making and cross-functional leadership.
```

```
I have a final-round interview at Anthropic for a research engineer role. Help me prep behavioral questions and suggest good questions to ask.
```

## Tips

- The more context you give, the better the output. Share your actual resume, not just a summary.
- Mention anything you know about the company or team beyond the job posting.
- Ask Claude to iterate—first drafts are starting points, not final answers.
- For cover letters, share any personal connection to the company's mission if one exists.
